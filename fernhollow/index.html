01Who I help

Three kinds of people sit across my desk most often.

  1. i.

    Working families

    A paycheck or two, a mortgage, kids, maybe a move or a new baby this year. You want it done correctly and you want to know nothing was missed.

    People ask“We both changed jobs. Is our withholding still right?”

  2. ii.

    People with income on the side

    Freelance work, a rental unit, a small shop run from the garage. I sort out what counts, what to keep track of, and what to set aside during the year.

    People ask“I got a form I’ve never seen before. What do I do with it?”

  3. iii.

    Retirees, and those close to it

    Pensions, savings withdrawals, Social Security, a home sale, or handling a parent’s paperwork. We go slowly and I explain every page before you sign.

    People ask“My husband always did the taxes. Where do I start?”

02When you first get in touch

  1. Today

  2. Within one business day

  3. When it suits you

04About Nora

A note from the person who will actually do your return.

This is a one-person office on purpose. When you call, you get me. When you come back next year, I already know about the rental, the daughter in college, and the box of receipts you’d rather not talk about.

I keep the client list small enough that I can read every return line by line and still return calls. I won’t use words you have to look up, and I won’t hand you something to sign until you understand it.

If your situation needs someone else — an attorney, a bookkeeper, a financial planner — I’ll say so and point you to one.

Nora Wickham

Nora Wickham · Owner, Fernhollow Tax Office
