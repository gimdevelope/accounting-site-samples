Questions

What people ask before they start.

Not on the list? Use the form below and ask it in your own words.

Do I have to come to the office?

No. Most clients never do. Papers come in by phone, questions go back and forth by text or email, and you sign on your screen. You are welcome to visit if you prefer paper.

What happens after I send the form?

June reads it and replies within one business day with a time to talk and a private upload link. The first conversation is short and is about your situation, not a sales pitch.

How do I know where my return stands?

Your status page shows one of four steps: received, in review, ready to sign, or filed. You also get a message each time it moves.

Is it safe to send photos of my forms?

You upload through a private link instead of email or text. Please never put a Social Security number in the contact form on this page.

I got a letter about a past return. Can you help?

Send us a photo of the letter. We will tell you what it is asking for and whether it is something we take on.
