01 — Practice areas

Four kinds of work. One file with your name on it.

Most clients start with one of these and end up using two or three. The partner who signs your return is the one who knows what happened in your books.

01

Tax returns

Federal and state returns for individuals, families and the businesses they own. Prepared in the office, reviewed by a partner, explained before you sign.

  • Personal and joint returns
  • Partnership, S-corp and small corporate returns
  • Estimated payments planned through the year
MWLed by Margaret Wetherlow IndividualsFamiliesBusinesses

02

Books & payroll

Monthly bookkeeping and payroll for local businesses that would rather not do either on a Sunday night.

  • Month-end close and readable statements
  • Paychecks, payroll filings, year-end forms
  • Sales tax filings, where they apply
DPLed by Daniel Pryce BusinessesNonprofits

03

Starting, selling or handing over a business

The numbers side of a big decision: which structure to start with, what a sale would look like, how to pass the shop to the next generation.

  • Choosing and setting up an entity
  • Buying in, buying out, bringing in family
  • Working alongside your attorney and banker
DPLed by Daniel Pryce OwnersFamilies

04

Letters from a tax agency

A notice arrives and it is not clear what it wants. Bring every page. We read it, tell you in plain words what it is asking for, and prepare the reply.

  • Federal and state notices
  • Requests for records or explanations
  • Returns from earlier years that need fixing

We can’t promise how an agency will answer. We can make sure the reply is complete and sent properly.

MWLed by Margaret Wetherlow IndividualsBusinesses
