05 Questions

What owners ask before they call.

For the first conversation, have handy:

  1. Last year's business tax return
  2. Your most recent bank statement
  3. The name of the software you use now, if any

Missing some of it? Come anyway.

I already have a bookkeeper. Do I have to replace them?

No. Plenty of owners keep the person who enters the bills. We do the close, the review, the cash page and the tax planning on top of their work.

My books are behind. Is that a problem?

It's a common starting point. We clean up the past months first, as a separate fixed-price project, and start the monthly rhythm once they're right.

What does it cost?

A fixed monthly fee, set after we've looked at your accounts, so you know the number before you sign anything. [Your monthly fee range goes here]

Which software do you work in?

The common small-business accounting programs. If you have one already and it fits, we stay in it. If it doesn't, we'll say why and handle the move.

Do you also file the tax returns?

Yes. Business and owner returns are prepared in our office from the books we've been closing all year, and the same partner signs off on both.
