02 How a month works

The same four steps, every month.

No surprises about what we need or when you'll hear from us. Your part takes a few minutes; ours takes the rest.

  1. Week 1

    You send, or we pull

    We download statements from the accounts you've connected. Snap a photo of anything on paper.

    You: a few minutes
  2. Week 2

    We sort and match

    Every transaction gets a category, and every account is checked against its statement.

    Us
  3. Week 3

    One short list of questions

    You: a quick reply
  4. Month-end

04 Questions

What owners ask before they start.

Don't see yours? Put it in the form. It's a fair first question.

What happens after I send the form?

One of us reads it and writes back within one business day. We'll ask two or three questions about your business, then suggest a short consultation to look at where your books stand.

You won't get a sales sequence or a stream of follow-up emails.

My books are months behind. Can you still help?

Yes. Catch-up is a common way to start. We go back to the last month that was closed properly and work forward, and we tell you up front how far back we need to go and what we'll need from you.

Do I have to switch software or banks?

Usually not. We start with what you already use and only suggest a change when it would clearly save you time. If we do, we handle the move and keep your history.

How do you price the work?

A flat monthly fee, based on how many accounts and transactions you have and which of the four jobs you hand over. You get it in writing before anything starts, and it doesn't change without a conversation first.
